# 0.8.6 — versionCode 22

Prepared 2026-11-04. Changes since 0.8.5 (versionCode 21). One change: the notification icon. It
was still the bell-with-power glyph from Notifier, the app this one started from. It is now the
Paramanu logo drawn as line art: the research building, the reactor exhaust stack and the reactor
with its atom. No server, database or Remote Config change.

## Technical, for the record

- `res/drawable/ic_notification.xml` is redrawn. It is used by `setSmallIcon` and by FCM's
  `default_notification_icon` in the manifest. Both show it as an alpha mask, so every part is a
  white stroke or fill over transparency. The old icon needed an evenOdd hole for its power symbol;
  this one doesn't.
- Upload with priority 0. Nothing here needs pushing.
- **Not yet seen on a device.** Rendered at 24, 48, 72 and 288px: clear at 72px (xxhdpi), but the
  windows and the atom blur together at 24px (mdpi). Worth checking on a test send: the status-bar
  glyph and the shade's small icon.
