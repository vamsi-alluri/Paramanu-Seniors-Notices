# Privacy Policy — Paramanu Seniors Notices

**Last updated: 16 October 2026**

This policy describes how the Android app **Paramanu Seniors Notices**
(`org.paramanuseniorshealth.notices`) handles information. The app is published on behalf of
Paramanu Seniors Health, which is responsible for the notices it delivers.

## In short

Paramanu Seniors Notices does not ask you for your name, telephone number, email address, postal address,
date of birth, or any health information, and it does not collect any of them. It has no
advertising and no advertising identifier, and nothing it collects is sold or shared for
marketing. The notices it delivers are the same public announcements published on
paramanuseniorshealth.org.

The app does measure how it is being used — which notices are opened, how long pictures and
documents take to arrive, and whether the app is opened from a notification. This is done to
find and fix problems, and the measurements are not connected to your name or to any account
of yours. The section "What the app measures" below lists the main things that are recorded.

## What the app stores on your phone

- The notices you have received, so you can read one again after dismissing it.
- Pictures and notice documents that came with them. Only the most recent are kept; older ones are
  deleted automatically.
- Your activation code, and which kinds of notice you have chosen to receive.

The notices and pictures stay on your phone and are not uploaded. Your activation code is sent when
you enter it, as described in the next section. Using **Reset this app** in Settings, or
uninstalling the app, removes all of it.

## What the app sends over the internet

**An anonymous account identifier.** When you enter your activation code, the app creates an
anonymous account with Google Firebase. This is a random identifier. It is not connected to your
name, your telephone number, your email address, or any account you may have with Google. The
identifier is stored alongside your activation code so that the organisation can confirm the code
has been used, and can stop notices to a particular phone if asked.

**Your activation code**, for the same purpose.

**A message registration for your app installation.** Google Firebase Cloud Messaging assigns an
identifier to this installation of the app so notices can be delivered to it. The app itself does
not read or store this identifier. Google's handling of it is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

**Ordinary connection information.** As with any app or website, your device's internet address is
visible to the servers it connects to while a notice is being fetched.

Apart from the measurements described in the next section, nothing else is sent. In
particular, the app does not ask for permission to see your location, and does not send your
contacts, your photographs, or an advertising identifier. (The next section explains the rough
location that Google works out for itself.)

## What the app measures

To find out whether the app is working properly on real phones, it records what happens as it
is used, and sends those records to Google Analytics for Firebase. The records are tied to a
random identifier created for this installation of the app. They are not tied to your name,
your telephone number, your email address, your activation code, or any Google account.

What is recorded:

- That a notice arrived, and that the app was opened — including whether you opened it by
  tapping the notification or from your home screen.
- Which notices were on screen and for how long, and when a picture or document was opened.
  Notices are recorded by their reference number only. The words of a notice are never sent.
- How long pictures and documents took to arrive, how large they were, and whether they
  failed.
- Whether the phone was on wifi or mobile data at the time, and whether it was roaming. The
  app does not record which network you are on, or your telephone number.
- What happened when an activation code was entered: whether it was accepted, and if not, the
  reason. The code itself is sent only for the purpose described above, never as part of
  these measurements.
- If the app stops unexpectedly, a technical report describing what went wrong. This goes to
  Google Crashlytics rather than Google Analytics, and also describes the phone it happened on.
- **Details Google adds itself.** The app does not send these and does not ask for permission
  to see your location, but Google's measurement service attaches them to what it receives: the
  phone's make and model, its Android version, the version of this app, and the phone's
  language; and roughly where the phone is, worked out from its internet connection — at least
  the country, and possibly the town or city. The full set of details Google attaches is for
  Google to define, so this list may not be complete.

This is a list of the main things recorded, not a guarantee that nothing else is attached by
Google's software. What the app itself never records: your contacts, your photographs, an
advertising identifier, the text of any notice, or anything you type other than whether an
activation code was accepted.

## What the app does not do

- No advertising, and no advertising identifier. The app does not request the Android advertising
  ID permission.
- No tracking of you as a person. The usage measurements described above are tied to a random
  identifier for this installation, not to you. The app never gives the measurements a user
  identifier of any kind, never includes your activation code in them, and records notices only
  by their reference number. So the measurements contain nothing that links them
  to the activation records.
- No sale or sharing of information with anyone for marketing.
- No medical advice, diagnosis, prescriptions, or appointment booking. The app delivers
  announcements only, and is not a substitute for contacting the dispensary or your doctor.

## Who processes this information

Google, through the Firebase service, on behalf of Paramanu Seniors Health. Messages are delivered
by Firebase Cloud Messaging; the activation record is held in a Firebase database located in
Singapore; usage measurements are processed by Google Analytics for Firebase and stored in
Google BigQuery in Mumbai, India; technical reports about unexpected stops are processed by
Google Crashlytics, which holds them on Google's own servers. Google's practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy at
https://policies.google.com/privacy and Firebase's data handling at
https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

## How long it is kept

Notices and pictures on your phone remain until you delete them, reset the app, or uninstall it.

The record connecting your activation code to its anonymous identifier is kept while the code is in
use. If you ask the organisation to release your code, or it is withdrawn, that record is removed.

Usage measurements are kept by Google Analytics for two months, which is the shortest period
it allows. The detailed records used for analysis are deleted automatically after sixty days.
Technical reports about unexpected stops are kept for ninety days.

## Your choices

- **Choose what you receive.** Settings lets you turn each kind of notice on or off.
- **Stop notices.** Turn off notifications for the app in Android Settings. This stops notices
  appearing, but the app's measurements (such as when it is opened, and any crash report) can
  still be recorded.
- **Stop everything.** Uninstall the app. Nothing more is then sent from your phone.
- **Remove your information.** **Reset this app** in Settings deletes your code and every saved
  notice from your phone. To have the activation record removed as well, contact the organisation
  using the details below.
- **Measurement.** The usage measurement described above is governed by a single switch held by
  the organisation, which applies to every phone, not one at a time. It is there to answer particular
  questions about how the app performs, and the organisation will turn it off once those
  questions are answered. The app does not offer a setting for it on your phone, and the
  organisation cannot exclude a single phone from it.
- **Deleting measurements.** Because the measurements contain no name or code that
  ties them to you, the organisation cannot pick out one person's records to delete them. What
  does happen is that collection can be stopped for every phone at once, at any time (a phone
  follows within a short time of the switch being turned off, not the same instant), and the
  records expire on their own: Google Analytics keeps them for two months and the detailed
  records are deleted after sixty days. If you want your phone to stop contributing, uninstall
  the app.

## Children

Paramanu Seniors Notices is intended for adults. It is not directed at children and is not designed to
appeal to them.
