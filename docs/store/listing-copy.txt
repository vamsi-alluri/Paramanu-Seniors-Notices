Paramanu Seniors Notices delivers announcements from Paramanu Seniors Health directly to
your phone, so you know about a change before you travel to the dispensary.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

- Dispensary and OPD timing changes
- Holiday and unscheduled closures
- Notices and circulars published by the organisation

Each notice arrives as a notification with a short headline you can read at a
glance. Tap it to open the full notice, which you can zoom in on to read
comfortably. Every notice you have received is kept in the app, so you can look
back at one you have already dismissed.

DESIGNED FOR OLDER READERS

The app uses large, high-contrast text and follows the text size you have already
set on your phone. There is nothing to configure and no account to remember.

GETTING STARTED

Collect an activation code from the dispensary counter and enter it once when you
first open the app. That is the only setup step. Notices begin arriving after it.

You can stop receiving notices at any time from within the app.

WHAT THIS APP DOES NOT DO

This app delivers announcements only. It does not provide medical advice,
diagnosis, treatment information, prescriptions or appointment booking, and it is
not a substitute for contacting the dispensary or your doctor.

PRIVACY

The app does not ask for your name, telephone number, email address or any health
information, and none is collected. The notices it delivers are the same public
announcements published on the Paramanu Seniors Health website.

CONTACT

For questions about a notice, please contact Paramanu Seniors Health directly.
