TITLE (max 30)
Paramanu Seniors Notices

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80)
Dispensary hours, closures and notices from Paramanu Seniors Health.

FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
Paramanu Seniors Notices delivers announcements from Paramanu Seniors Health directly to your phone, so you know about a change before you travel to the dispensary.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

- Dispensary and OPD timing changes
- Holiday and unscheduled closures
- Notices and circulars published by the organisation

Your dispensary's address, timings and helpdesk number are shown at the top of the app and stay up to date.

Each notice arrives as a notification with a short headline you can read at a glance. Tap it to open the app at that notice. Every notice you have received is kept in the app, so you can read it again whenever you want. Pictures can be opened full screen and zoomed, and circulars open in your phone's PDF reader.

DESIGNED FOR OLDER READERS

The app uses large, high-contrast text and follows the text size you have already set on your phone. There is nothing to configure and no account to remember.

GETTING STARTED

Collect an activation code from the dispensary counter and enter it once when you first open the app. That is the only setup step. Notices begin arriving after it.

To stop receiving notices, turn off notifications for this app in your phone's settings, or uninstall it.

WHAT THIS APP DOES NOT DO

This app delivers announcements only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment information, prescriptions or appointment booking, and it is not a substitute for contacting the dispensary or your doctor.

PRIVACY

The app does not ask for your name, telephone number, email address or any health information. The notices it delivers are announcements from Paramanu Seniors Health about the dispensary.

CONTACT

For questions about a notice, please contact Paramanu Seniors Health directly.
